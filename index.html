LMB shoots. Space jumps.
Jump shots are the best shot in the game, if you time them. The ball leaves at your arm's speed plus your body's. Shoot at the top of your jump and it flies true. Too early and it sails long, too late and it drops short, and drifting pushes it sideways. The ring tightens and goes green at the top. Set shots from the floor are fine up close but get shaky with distance.
