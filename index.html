Basketball Game

A first-person shooting game where the ball physics decide everything: drag, backspin (Magnus lift), and friction on the rim, backboard, and floor.
  • Hold LMB to charge launch speed, release to shoot. The ball leaves along your crosshair.
  • Hold RMB to aim: zooms in, slows you down, and tightens your spread a lot.
  • Moving or jumping makes you less accurate, and your momentum carries into the ball.
  • Beyond the arc = 3 points, inside = 2. Walk over loose balls to pick them up, or press R for a new one.
